# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to a positive, inclusive, and collaborative environment.

## Our Standards

* **Be respectful.** Use welcoming and inclusive language.
* **Be considerate.** Assume best intentions. Give the benefit of the doubt.
* **Be collaborative.** Work together to resolve disagreements constructively.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. Unacceptable behavior includes:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
* Trolling, insulting or harassing language.
* Discriminatory jokes and language.
* Public or private harassment.
* Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers are obligated to uphold this Code of Conduct.
